About company

Diversifying a legacy into a multi-dimensional business.

BGS Industrial Solution is a new venture founded by Mr Bharat Khanna, Mr Gaurav Khanna and Mr Shubham Khanna (Directors).

It is a sister concern of Bharat DyeChem Pvt Ltd, a market leader in the textile industry for the last 70 years. BGS was started with the idea to diversify and create a multi-dimensional business catering to the Automotive, Electronics, Steel, Rubber, Glass, Furniture, Construction, Railway and Sanitary industries.

With the support of Stickova Adhesives and Pidilite Inc, we aim to provide the best quality products at a competitive price.
